# SmartSleep

Sleep and snore tracker for iPhone and Apple Watch.

**Support:** giangnd.smc@gmail.com

## Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

**Summary.** SmartSleep processes sleep data on your device. The app has no server of its own, requires no account, shows no ads and includes no third-party tracking or analytics tools.

**Microphone audio.** When tracking is on, the microphone is used to recognise snoring, sleep talking, coughing and the noise level in the room. The analysis happens on the device. The app keeps only short recordings (about 6 seconds) around moments when snoring or speech is detected, so you can listen back. These clips stay in the app's storage, are deleted automatically after 30 days, and you can turn saving off or delete them all in Settings. The app does not send audio anywhere.

**Movement.** The phone's motion sensor (and Apple Watch, if you use one) is used to count how often you move. This data is stored only on the device.

**Apple Health.** If you allow it, SmartSleep writes your sleep periods to Apple Health, and reads sleep data and heart rate from it to show in reports. Health data is not used for advertising and is not shared with anyone else. You can withdraw permission at any time in the Health app.

**Notifications.** Bedtime reminders, alarms and other reminders are local notifications created by the device itself.

**Purchases.** Purchases of SmartSleep Pro are handled by Apple through your Apple account. The app only receives from Apple which plan is active; it does not receive your name, email or payment details.

**Backup.** A backup file is created only when you tap Back up, and is saved where you choose. It contains each night's numbers, not the audio clips.

**Deleting data.** You can delete individual nights in History, delete all recordings in Settings, or remove the app to delete all of its data from the device.

**Children.** The app is not directed at children under 13.

**Changes.** When this policy changes, the new version will be in the app with its update date.

**Contact.** Questions about this policy: giangnd.smc@gmail.com

## Terms of Use

**Not a medical device.** SmartSleep is a tool for tracking sleep habits. Scores, snoring minutes, quiet gaps while snoring and advice are estimates from the phone's microphone and sensors. They are not a diagnosis and do not replace a doctor's opinion. If you are worried about your health, see a doctor.

**Alarm.** The smart alarm depends on the app still running, the battery, the volume and the device's notification settings. For anything important, set a backup alarm as well.

**Subscriptions.** Monthly and yearly plans renew automatically until you cancel. Payment is charged to your Apple account when you confirm the purchase, and on renewal within 24 hours before the end of the period. Cancel in your Apple ID Settings at least 24 hours before the renewal date. The lifetime plan is paid once and does not renew. Refunds are handled by Apple.

**Free trial.** If a plan includes a free trial, you will be charged when it ends unless you cancelled before then.

**Your content.** Your sleep data and recordings are yours and stay on your device. You are responsible when recording in a room where other people sleep.
